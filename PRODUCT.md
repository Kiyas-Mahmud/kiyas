# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, research labs, and collaborators visiting Kiyas Mahmud's personal
portfolio. Typical session: 60 to 90 seconds on a laptop, judging credibility,
skimming projects and publications, then deciding whether to reach out. They
arrive via GitHub, Google Scholar, or a shared link, often in the evening on a
dimly lit screen.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for an ML researcher and AI engineer. It exists to make
credibility felt fast: publications, a national ICT award, and shipped projects
should land within the first scroll. Success looks like a CV download, a GitHub
visit, or a contact message.

## Brand Personality

Precise and engineered, calm and premium, with bold experimental moments and a
warm human undertone. The interface should feel like peering into a calm,
luminous instrument panel: quiet confidence, nothing shouting, one memorable
flourish per fold.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template look (centered hero, identical icon-card grids, purple
  gradient on white).
- The previous version's hacker/terminal costume (glitch text, matrix rain,
  scanlines). That site already exists; this one must not repeat it.
- Beige ultra-minimalism with tiny text and no color commitment.
- Cluttered maximalism with competing animations per section.

## Design Principles

1. Credibility in the first scroll. Awards, publications, and projects lead;
   decoration follows.
2. Glass is structural, not decorative. Frosted panels exist only where content
   floats over the aurora light field.
3. One idea per fold. Long scroll, deliberate pacing, generous separation.
4. Motion is orchestration, not noise. One choreographed load, then quiet
   scroll-driven reveals.
5. Warmth through words. Precise layouts, human copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard baseline: prefers-reduced-motion support for all animation, full
keyboard navigation, visible focus states, descriptive alt text, and semantic
landmarks.
